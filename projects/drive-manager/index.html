Drive Manager

A simple and easy-to-use Google Drive manager for Docs & Sheets.

Access your Google Drive files directly from the sidebar in Google Docs and Sheets. Search for files and folders, and open them without leaving your document.

Features

  • Search files and folders in Google Drive
  • Quick access from the sidebar panel
  • Open files directly from Docs or Sheets
  • Works with Google Workspace
