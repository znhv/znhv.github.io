Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 27, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Drive Manager for Docs & Sheets ("the Add-on") uses and protects your information when you use it within Google Docs and Google Sheets.

1. No Data Collection

This Add-on does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, file content, or user metadata. All operations are performed locally using the official Google Apps Script environment within your Google Workspace session.

2. OAuth Scopes and Permissions

The Add-on uses limited Google OAuth scopes solely for functionality:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui — used to render the sidebar UI inside Google Docs/Sheets.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly — used to display your Drive file list and structure.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly — used to open Drive files in a new tab at your request.

No OAuth tokens or file contents are stored or transmitted externally.

3. Third-Party Access

The Add-on does not use or integrate with any third-party APIs or services. All interactions remain entirely within Google's secure infrastructure.

4. Cookies and Tracking

This Add-on does not use cookies, analytics, tracking scripts, or any third-party monitoring tools.

5. Children's Privacy

The Add-on is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Security

Since no user data is collected or stored, the Add-on minimizes security risks. All operations are executed within Google's trusted execution environment.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Add-on evolves. Users will be notified of any material changes via the Chrome Web Store or Workspace Marketplace listing.
