Privacy Policy

Updated: January 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Notifications add-on for Google Forms handles your information.

1. Data Collection

The Notifications add-on collects and stores the following information:

  • User Email: Your email address is collected to create a unique user identifier and authenticate with our backend.
  • Notification Configurations: Your notification settings and rules (such as trigger conditions, recipient emails, and notification preferences) are stored on our backend server to enable the notification functionality across sessions.
  • Form Response Triggers: The add-on detects when new responses are submitted to your forms to trigger notifications, but does not store the actual form response content.

Notifications are sent through your Gmail account using the gmail.send scope. We do not collect or store the actual content of your Google Forms responses or notification emails.

2. Data Access

Notifications requires the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/forms.currentonly – to access the current Google Form and detect new responses that trigger notifications.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send – to send notification emails through your Gmail account when form responses are received.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui – to render the user interface inside Google Forms.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.external_request – to communicate with our backend servers for storing and retrieving notification configurations.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.scriptapp – to enable core Apps Script functionality required for the add-on to operate.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email – to identify your account and associate notification configurations with your user profile.

These scopes are used exclusively to enable notification features. The add-on does not access or read the actual content of form responses beyond what is necessary to trigger notifications.

3. Data Storage

User data is stored securely on our backend servers:

  • Your email address is used to create a unique user ID that is linked to your notification configurations.
  • Notification configurations are stored securely in our database.
  • Authentication is performed using OpenID Connect tokens to ensure secure communication between the add-on and our backend servers.
  • The add-on uses the script.external_request scope to communicate with our backend API for storing and retrieving your configurations.

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. Form response content and notification emails are not stored on our servers.

4. Data Sharing

Notifications does not sell or share your personal information or notification configurations with third parties, except as required to provide the service (such as sending notification emails) or as required by law.

5. Use of Google User Data

The add-on's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Retention

Your notification configurations are retained as long as you continue to use the service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Notifications add-on, please contact us at znhv@yandex.com.
