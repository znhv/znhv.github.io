Grafium

iOS client for Grafana.

Grafium is a simple, focused iOS app for checking your Grafana dashboards on the go. Connect your own Grafana instances, keep an eye on key panels and alerts, and jump between servers whenever you need to — all from your iPhone.

Whether you’re on the way home, in a meeting, or away from your laptop, Grafium helps you quickly see what’s going on with your services without digging through a browser.

What you can do with Grafium

  • View your Grafana dashboards from a clean, mobile-friendly interface
  • Open individual panels to inspect important metrics in more detail
  • Keep key panels in Favorites for quick access
  • Switch between multiple Grafana servers and environments
  • Optionally upgrade to Grafium Pro for unlimited servers, favorites, and early access to new features
