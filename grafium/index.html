Privacy

Your servers stay yours.

The free app has no backend at all. Grafium Pro adds a small relay for push notifications that never sees your server’s address, credentials or metrics. Read the Privacy Policy.

Read-only by design

Grafium never edits dashboards, panels or alert rules. The one change it can make is the one you ask for: setting up its contact point for Pro alerts.

Secrets in the Keychain

Tokens and passwords live in the iOS Keychain, and showing a saved one takes Face ID or your passcode.

No account, no tracking

No sign-up, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs. The free app talks to your Grafana and nothing else.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there a Grafana app for iPhone?

Yes. Grafium is an independent, native iPhone app for Grafana dashboards and alerts. It connects to the Grafana servers you already run and draws every panel with native iOS charts instead of a web view. It is not made by Grafana Labs.

Does Grafium work with self-hosted Grafana?

Yes, that is what it is built for. Grafium works with Grafana 9 or later that your iPhone can reach over HTTPS with a trusted certificate. Self-signed certificates are not supported.

Can I get Grafana alerts as push notifications on iPhone?

Yes, with Grafium Pro. A firing alert arrives as a Time Sensitive notification, and when it resolves the same notification quietly updates. Push notifications need Grafana 12 or later; one tap in the app sets up the Grafium contact point on your server.

Are there Grafana widgets for the iPhone Home Screen?

Yes, with Grafium Pro: a panel’s value or chart and your firing alerts on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen and in StandBy, plus a Grafium Alerts button in Control Center. Widgets refresh about every 15–60 minutes, as iOS allows, and show when they were last updated.

Which panel types does Grafium show?

22 of Grafana’s 25 built-in visualizations, including time series, stat, gauge, bar gauge, bar chart, table, heatmap, logs, state timeline, status history, pie chart, geomap and text. A panel type it can’t draw shows its raw data instead.

Will Grafium change anything on my Grafana server?

No. Grafium only reads: it never edits dashboards, panels or alert rules. The one change it can make is the one you ask for in Grafium Pro: creating, updating or removing its own contact point.

How do I sign in to my Grafana?

With an API or service account token, a username and password, LDAP, JWT, an auth proxy, your organization’s SSO page, or anonymously. Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Is my data private?

The free app has no backend, no account, no analytics and no tracking: requests go straight from your iPhone to your Grafana. Grafium Pro’s push relay stores only push tokens, a random channel ID and your subscription status, and never receives your server’s address, credentials, dashboards or metrics.

How much does Grafium cost?

Grafium is free. Grafium Pro, with instant alerts and widgets, is $3.99 a month with a 1-week free trial or $29.99 a year with a 2-week free trial (US prices).

What do I need to run Grafium?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. To try it without a server, tap Try Demo: Grafium opens live dashboards on Grafana Labs’ public demo server.
