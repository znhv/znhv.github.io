Grafium

A native, read-only iPhone client for Grafana® dashboards and alerts.

Grafium connects to the Grafana servers you already run and shows your dashboards and alerts on your iPhone. Panels are drawn natively — there is no embedded web view and no server-side image rendering, so a dashboard opens as an iOS screen rather than a page zoomed to fit.

The app reads. It never writes to your Grafana: no edits, no saves, no silences, no annotations.

What you can do

  • Browse folders and dashboards, and search across them
  • Open any panel full screen and scrub through its values
  • Pin the dashboards and folders you check often; recently opened ones stay to hand
  • See firing and pending alert rules, with their instances and labels
  • Keep several servers side by side and switch between them
  • Connect with an API token, username and password, LDAP, JWT, an auth proxy, SSO, or anonymously

Try it without a server

Tap Try Demo on the first screen. Grafium connects anonymously to play.grafana.org, the public demo server run by Grafana Labs, so you can see real dashboards and live alerts before entering anything of your own.

Privacy and price

Grafium is free. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no ads. It has no backend of its own: requests go straight from your iPhone to your Grafana server. Credentials are held in the iOS Keychain and can be revealed only behind Face ID. Nothing is tracked, logged or sent anywhere else — see the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 26 or later, and a Grafana 9 or newer server you can already reach.
