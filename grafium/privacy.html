Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · replaces the version of September 9, 2026

In short. The free app has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no backend: it talks only to the Grafana® servers you add. Grafium Pro’s push notifications go through a small relay run by the developer. The relay stores push tokens, a random channel ID and your subscription status — never your server’s address, your credentials, your dashboards or your metrics.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how the Grafium app for iPhone (“Grafium”, “the App”) handles information. Grafium is made by an independent developer, Boris Zhenikhov (“the developer”, “we”). It is not made by Grafana Labs.

2. What stays on your iPhone

The following is written only to your iPhone, inside storage that belongs to Grafium:

  • Connection details — the address of each Grafana server you add and the name you give it.
  • Credentials — the API token, password or JWT you enter is kept in the iOS Keychain, never in a file, a log or the App’s settings. Showing a saved credential takes Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  • Session cookie — if you sign in through your organization’s SSO page, the resulting Grafana session cookie is kept the same way, for that server only.
  • Preferences — pinned and recently opened dashboards, and per-dashboard settings such as the time range.
  • A cache of dashboard structure — the layout your server returned, so a dashboard you revisit opens without waiting. It holds no credentials.

3. What leaves your iPhone

The free app makes network requests in these situations only:

  • To your own Grafana servers. Read requests carrying the credential you configured for that server. This traffic goes directly between your iPhone and infrastructure you control.
  • To Grafana Labs’ public demo server (play.grafana.org), only if you tap Try Demo. The connection is anonymous.
  • To a news feed, only when a dashboard you open contains a Grafana “news” panel. The App fetches the address that panel specifies (by default grafana.com/blog/news.xml) without any credential or cookie.
  • To images and links your dashboards reference. Images are fetched without a credential; links open in Safari, outside the App.

If you use Grafium Pro, two more parties are involved: Apple, which processes your subscription and delivers notifications, and the Grafium relay described in the next section. The servers you reach apply their own privacy practices; for play.grafana.org and the default news feed that is Grafana Labs.

4. Grafium Pro and push notifications

Push notifications deliver alerts from your Grafana servers to your phone. To do that, Grafium uses a small relay server operated by the developer on Cloudflare Workers.

What the relay stores

Only what is needed to deliver notifications to the right devices:

  • the push notification token of each device that turned on push notifications, and whether it is a development or production token;
  • if you added the Firing Alerts widget, the push token Apple gives that widget, so the widget can refresh as soon as an alert fires or resolves;
  • a channel identifier and the secret your Grafana server signs alerts with. Both are random values generated on your device; they do not contain your server’s address;
  • the identifier of your App Store subscription (the “original transaction ID”), its environment (Sandbox or Production), its status and its expiry date, as signed by Apple.

What passes through without being stored

When your Grafana server sends an alert, the relay reads the rule name and its summary (or description) and forwards them to Apple Push Notification service, which delivers them to your devices. They are not written to storage or logs. Everything else in the alert — labels, values, links, images — is discarded on arrival.

What never reaches the developer

Your Grafana server’s address, your login, password, token or session, your dashboards and your metrics. The App talks to your Grafana server directly; the relay only receives what your Grafana server chooses to send to it.

Your Grafium key

The link and secret for each server are derived on your device from a random key kept in your iCloud Keychain, so they stay the same on your other devices. The key is never sent to the developer.

Processors

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the relay and its database. Apple delivers notifications and processes payments; the developer never receives your payment details or your Apple Account identity.

Logs

The relay keeps no request logs. Cloudflare’s dashboard shows the developer aggregate counts (requests, errors, processing time) without request contents.

5. Grafium Pro widgets

Widgets and the Control Center button ask your Grafana server directly, the same way the App does; nothing passes through the relay. To show something while the App is closed, they keep on your device, in storage shared only by Grafium and its widgets: the list of your servers (names and addresses, without passwords or tokens), the dashboard filters you last picked, and the last value or alert list each widget showed, with its time. Your Grafana credentials stay in the device Keychain; widgets can read them after you first unlock the device following a restart, and they never leave the device.

6. What Grafium does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No tracking, no advertising identifiers, no data brokers. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs — the App is built on Apple’s own frameworks.
  • No account, no sign-up and no email address required.
  • No changes to your Grafana, with one exception you start yourself: with Grafium Pro, choosing Automatic as a server’s contact point creates, updates or removes the Grafium contact point on that server.

7. Retention and deletion

  • A device token is deleted from the relay when Apple reports it invalid, or when you turn off Alerts for a server on that device.
  • A channel is deleted when you delete the server in Grafium.
  • Subscription records stay while the subscription exists.
  • On your iPhone, deleting a server removes its details and erases its credential from the Keychain; Settings ▸ Clear Cache discards the stored dashboard structure; deleting the App removes everything it stored, including Keychain items. Removing a widget or a server stops widget reads.

8. Security

Credentials are held in the iOS Keychain, encrypted by the system and protected by your device passcode. Connections use HTTPS with the system’s standard certificate validation. Alerts reach the relay only when signed with your channel’s secret. Grafium is only as private as the servers you point it at: how your Grafana instance handles its data is up to whoever operates it.

9. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data a company holds about you, and to know whether it is sold or shared. The free app holds nothing about you outside your device. For Grafium Pro, the relay holds only the pseudonymous records described above, with no name or email attached; the quickest way to delete them is to turn off Alerts or delete the server in the App. You can also write to the address below, and we will help as far as we can identify your records. Nothing is sold or shared.

10. Children

Grafium is a tool for people who run or monitor computer systems. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from them.
